Welcome to the Tiny Toss Dice Game!

The game has 6 rounds in total where players take turns rolling the dice. You only score points when you roll a 6 🎯 — any other number means no points for that round. After all 6 rounds are finished, the player with the most sixes wins the game! 🏆
